# Output Format

## A. Research summary

Include:

- Research date
- Semrush database
- Competitor domains and URLs researched
- Keyword Gap scope
- Data limitations

## B. SEO brief

Include:

- Business objective
- Audience
- Search intent
- Primary keyword and selection rationale
- Secondary, supporting, FAQ, and conversion terms
- Rejected close alternatives and reasons
- Recommended title, meta description, and slug
- Article type and outline
- Competitor format patterns
- Content gaps to address
- WooshPay messages, internal links, CTA, and verification items

## C. Clean article

Deliver:

1. SEO title
2. Meta description
3. URL slug
4. H1
5. Publish-ready article
6. FAQ when justified
7. CTA
8. Suggested internal links

Do not include keyword labels in the clean article.

## D. Keyword annotation report

Provide this table:

| Keyword | Classification | Intent | Volume | Difficulty | Article locations |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|

Then provide an annotated copy using:

- `[PRIMARY: keyword]`
- `[SECONDARY: keyword]`
- `[SUPPORTING: term]`
- `[FAQ: question]`

Annotate actual occurrences only. Do not add repetitions to improve counts.

## E. Final audit

List:

- Competitors researched
- Content gaps addressed
- Claims requiring verification
- Potential cannibalization
- Missing Semrush data
- Suggested post-publication measurements

When producing Word documents, create separate clean and annotated documents unless the user requests a combined file.
