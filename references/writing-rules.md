# WooshPay Singapore SEO Writing Rules

## Reader and language

- Default to Singapore/British English.
- Explain specialist payment terminology on first use.
- Match the sophistication of the stated audience.
- Use SGD and Singapore-specific examples only when accurate and relevant.

## Structure

- Give a direct answer or useful orientation in the opening.
- Use one H1 and a logical H2/H3 hierarchy.
- Make headings descriptive rather than clickbait.
- Use comparison tables for real decision criteria.
- Add FAQs only when supported by keyword, PAA, or reader research.
- End with a next step appropriate to the search intent.

## Originality and information gain

- Synthesize patterns across competitors instead of mirroring one article.
- Add information competitors omit, underserve, or leave outdated.
- Prefer practical decision guidance, examples, constraints, and trade-offs.
- Never present invented first-hand experience.

## Keyword use

- Place the primary keyword naturally in the SEO title, H1, opening, and an appropriate subheading when possible.
- Use secondary terms only where they improve meaning.
- Use close variants naturally; do not repeat exact phrases mechanically.
- Avoid fixed density targets and keyword stuffing.

## Evidence and risk

- Cite authoritative sources for regulations, statistics, market facts, and payment rules.
- Prefer official regulators, government sources, standards bodies, and first-party documentation.
- Add `{{VERIFY: ...}}` when a publishable claim cannot be confirmed.
- Do not make legal, regulatory, security, or financial guarantees.

## Style

- Avoid generic AI introductions and filler conclusions.
- Keep paragraphs focused and scannable.
- Prefer specific nouns and verbs over promotional adjectives.
- Do not use competitor trademarks in a misleading way.
- Do not closely paraphrase source material.
