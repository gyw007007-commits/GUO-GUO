# Semrush Research Protocol

## Required market

Default to the Singapore database. Record the database and research date in the final report.

## Research sequence

1. Analyze every supplied competitor domain with Organic Research.
2. Analyze every supplied competitor article URL when URL-level data is available.
3. Run Keyword Gap against WooshPay when the WooshPay domain and comparison data are available.
4. Inspect the SERP for the strongest candidate keywords.
5. Cluster keywords by shared intent and likely target page.

Collect these fields when available:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Keyword | Candidate query |
| Intent | Informational, commercial, transactional, or navigational |
| Singapore volume | Local demand |
| Keyword difficulty | Ranking feasibility |
| CPC | Commercial signal, not a direct SEO score |
| Position | Competitor's current ranking |
| Ranking URL | Page earning the ranking |
| SERP features | PAA, snippet, local pack, video, and other formats |

Use `Not available` for missing data. Never infer a precise metric.

## Filtering

Remove or deprioritize:

- Terms unrelated to WooshPay products or the target reader
- Terms whose location intent is outside Singapore
- High-volume terms with weak business relevance
- Brand terms belonging to unrelated companies
- Duplicates and close variants that belong on the same page
- Terms likely to cannibalize an existing WooshPay page

## Selection rule

Prioritize in this order:

1. WooshPay business relevance
2. Search-intent fit
3. Ranking feasibility
4. Conversion potential
5. Search volume

Document why the primary keyword was selected and why close alternatives were rejected.

## Competitor safeguards

Use competitor content as research evidence, not source copy. Extract patterns, entities, questions, and gaps. Do not reproduce their wording, proprietary graphics, unique examples, or unsupported claims.
