# GUO GUO SEO

Create original, publish-ready SEO articles for WooshPay Singapore. Use competitors to understand search intent, keyword coverage, effective structure, and information gaps. Never copy or closely paraphrase competitor wording.

## Workflow

Complete these phases in order:

1. Collect the brief.
2. Research competitors and keywords with Semrush.
3. Select and cluster keywords.
4. Analyze competing article structures.
5. Create an SEO content brief.
6. Write the article.
7. Run keyword, originality, factual, and brand QA.
8. Produce clean and annotated deliverables.

Do not draft the article before completing the research and brief.

## 1. Collect the brief

Ask only for missing information. Ask in short rounds instead of presenting a long questionnaire.

First establish:

- Topic or seed keyword
- Business objective
- Target Singapore audience
- Competitor article URLs
- Competitor brands or domains
- WooshPay product or landing page to promote
- Language

Then establish only details needed for delivery:

- Preferred word count
- Required CTA
- Internal pages to link
- Desired output format
- Whether brief approval is required before drafting

Proceed when the objective, audience, topic, and competitors are known. Never ask again for information already supplied.

## 2. Research with Semrush

Read [references/semrush-research.md](references/semrush-research.md). Use the Singapore database unless the user requests another market.

Use Semrush tools when connected. If unavailable, request a Semrush export or clearly label non-Semrush estimates. Never invent metrics.

Analyze both domains and specific article URLs when supplied. Run Keyword Gap when WooshPay and competitor domains are available.

## 3. Select keywords

Classify the researched terms as:

- One primary keyword
- Three to eight secondary keywords
- Supporting semantic terms
- FAQ or PAA questions
- Conversion terms

Prioritize business relevance, search-intent fit, ranking feasibility, conversion potential, and then search volume. Do not select a keyword solely for volume.

Check existing WooshPay pages for likely keyword cannibalization. Report uncertainty when site data is unavailable.

## 4. Analyze competing content

For each competitor article, capture:

- Title, meta description, and URL
- H1/H2/H3 hierarchy
- Content type and dominant search intent
- Opening-answer pattern
- Tables, lists, tools, examples, and FAQs
- CTA type and placement
- Topics and entities covered
- Weak, outdated, or missing information
- Opportunities for WooshPay to add original value

Derive a structure from cross-competitor patterns and content gaps. Imitate useful formats only. Never copy sentences, distinctive phrasing, examples, data, or conclusions.

## 5. Create the SEO brief

Include:

- Search intent and target reader
- Primary, secondary, supporting, FAQ, and conversion keywords
- Recommended title, meta description, and URL slug
- Recommended content type
- H1/H2/H3 outline
- Questions the article must answer
- Competitor patterns worth adopting
- Information-gain opportunities
- Required WooshPay messages
- Internal and authoritative external link suggestions
- CTA
- Claims requiring verification

Continue to drafting unless the user explicitly requests approval of the brief first.

## 6. Write the article

Read [references/wooshpay-brand.md](references/wooshpay-brand.md) and [references/writing-rules.md](references/writing-rules.md) before drafting.

Write for Singapore readers. Default to Singapore/British English unless requested otherwise.

Answer the main query near the beginning. Satisfy the search intent before promoting WooshPay. Add specific, useful information and use tables only when they improve comparison or decision-making.

Use keywords naturally. Do not optimize toward a fixed keyword-density target. Never fabricate personal experience, customer results, statistics, prices, regulatory claims, or product capabilities. Mark unverified claims with `{{VERIFY: claim | suggested source}}`.

## 7. Quality assurance

Verify that:

- The article satisfies the dominant search intent.
- It is not a paraphrased competitor article.
- Each section adds distinct value.
- Keywords appear naturally and headings are coherent.
- WooshPay statements comply with the brand reference.
- Numbers and regulated-payment claims have sources or verification markers.
- The CTA fits the audience and intent.
- Information gaps identified in research are actually addressed.
- Cannibalization risk is reported.

Revise the article when a required check fails.

## 8. Deliver

Read [references/output-format.md](references/output-format.md). Always provide both a clean publish-ready article and a keyword annotation report. Produce a `.docx` version when requested and preserve the same content structure.
